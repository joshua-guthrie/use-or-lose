# Use or Lose Android acceptance plan

These cases define the pass conditions for the [parity matrix](feature-parity.md) and storage release gates. Use synthetic data, a fixed local date, and the [pinned desktop source](../specification.md#product-and-reference-build) to compute reference results. Run the phone cases on an emulator first and repeat the system handoffs and accessibility checks on a physical phone when a build is available.

Use the [milestone QA loop](../chapters/05-quality/05-02-quality-gates.md#run-the-loop-in-every-implementation-milestone) to select cases, record failures and fixes, and close each milestone only after all applicable gates pass.

## Calendar balance fixture

Set the app's local Today to **January 16, 2026**. Initialize a manual 2026 leave year starting January 11 with 26 pay periods, 100 h annual and 20 h sick starting balances, and 6 h annual accrual per period. The reference store's sick accrual is 4 h. Use the generated schedule without a payday offset override.

| Effective date | Annual leave used | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| January 13 | 8 h | Approved |
| January 16 | 2 h | Approved |
| January 22 | 4 h | Planned |
| January 25 | 3 h | Planned |

The first period runs **January 11–24**, accrues on January 24, and pays on January 30. Expected results from the reference build's date and month logic are recorded here.

| Look-up date | Annual | Sick | Case |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| January 13 | 92 h | 20 h | A historical date includes the 8 h entry that lands on it. |
| January 16 | 90 h | 20 h | Today includes the additional 2 h entry. |
| January 22 | 86 h | 20 h | The dated 4 h use applies. The first accrual has yet to post. |
| January 24 | 92 h | 24 h | The period closes after adding 6 h annual and 4 h sick. The period used 14 h annual and 0 h sick. |
| January 25 | 89 h | 24 h | The next period's 3 h use applies after the first close. |
| January 4, 2027 | 233 h | 120 h | The date belongs to the 2026 leave year. Accrual for its last pay period falls later. |

### Reproduce the desktop reference

Use these examples on Linux with the CLI on your path. The environment variable must point to a disposable directory. Reference tool names are for developers writing tests. The Android UI uses the app's own labels.

```bash
fixture_dir="$(mktemp -d)"
use-or-lose init --year 2026 --leave-year-start 2026-01-11 --pay-periods 26 \
  --annual-accrual 6 --annual-start 100 --sick-start 20 --data-dir "$fixture_dir"
use-or-lose add --year 2026 --date 2026-01-13 --category annual --used 8 --status approved --data-dir "$fixture_dir"
use-or-lose add --year 2026 --date 2026-01-16 --category annual --used 2 --status approved --data-dir "$fixture_dir"
use-or-lose add --year 2026 --date 2026-01-22 --category annual --used 4 --status planned --data-dir "$fixture_dir"
use-or-lose add --year 2026 --date 2026-01-25 --category annual --used 3 --status planned --data-dir "$fixture_dir"
use-or-lose balance --year 2026 --as-of 2026-01-24 --json --data-dir "$fixture_dir"
use-or-lose pay-period --year 2026 --date 2026-01-24 --json --data-dir "$fixture_dir"
use-or-lose month --year 2026 --month 1 --json --data-dir "$fixture_dir"
use-or-lose balance --as-of 2027-01-04 --json --data-dir "$fixture_dir"
```

Substitute each date in the expected-results table for the balance command. The month output provides period totals and date markers on any day the commands are run. Its Today field uses the runner's real date. Freeze the calculation clock to January 16 to compare that field directly. The desktop reference checks verified the dated values, inferred leave year, frozen Today snapshot, matching period close, and distinct payday and period-end markers. JSON store hashes matched before and after the dated and period reads.

The committed input store, golden outputs and fixture manifest are under [`android/qa/fixtures/calendar-2026`](../../qa/fixtures/calendar-2026) and [`android/qa/golden/calendar-2026`](../../qa/golden/calendar-2026). Regenerate them with `python android/qa/fixtures/build_calendar_fixture.py`; do not hand-edit the generated files. The manifest records the source revision, frozen clock, tree digest, requests and exact expected results. [Fixture manifest](../../qa/fixtures/calendar-2026.manifest.json)
