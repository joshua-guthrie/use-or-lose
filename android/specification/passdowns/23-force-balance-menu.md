# Force Balance in the Calendar menu

Open **Calendar → Menu → Force Balance** to set a verified balance for one category and date. The standard Android date picker comes first. Choose the leave type, enter the target and reason, then review and confirm the adjustment. Earlier clocking entries remain in the ledger. The same screens are available from maintenance. [Requirements and mockups](../chapters/01-calendar/01-06-force-balance.md)

I10 connects both routes. Verify same-date reapplication, zero targets on accrual dates, cancellation, changed previews, preservation of original entries and the return to the original Calendar state. [Acceptance case](../verification/acceptance-tests.md#ledger-and-leave-year-acceptance)

The desktop adjustment fixture reproduced 5 h, 8 h and 20 h targets. Reapplication replaced only the previous adjustment. Earlier results and original IDs and comments stayed intact.
