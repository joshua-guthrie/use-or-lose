# Analytics with one, six, or eleven months

The Seasonality and Cumulative Usage mockups show the same synthetic year after one, six, and eleven months. The totals are 10, 52, and 94 h. Later dates retain their place on the axis. The chart distinguishes a recorded zero from a date beyond the cutoff and carries its values into full-screen mode. [Six annotated mockups](../chapters/03-reports-data/03-02-report-screens.md#analytics-with-one-six-or-eleven-months)
