# Calendar date markers

Look to the right of the date number on Calendar.

| Icon | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| <img src="../artwork/markers/holiday.png" width="9" height="9" alt="Star"> | **Holiday.** Open the day to see the holiday name. |
| <img src="../artwork/markers/payday.png" width="9" height="9" alt="Coin"> | **Payday.** Scheduled pay date. |
| <img src="../artwork/markers/pay-period-end.png" width="9" height="9" alt="Flag"> | **Pay-period end.** Last day of the pay period. |
| <img src="../artwork/markers/leave-year-end.png" width="9" height="9" alt="Checked calendar"> | **Leave-year end.** Last day of the leave year. |

A date can have several markers. Open the day to read all its event names.
