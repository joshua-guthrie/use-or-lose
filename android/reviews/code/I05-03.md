## Notes

- **Picker state machine conformance (verified in code and JVM tests).** `onChooseDate` initializes `pickerDraft`/`pickerMonth`/`pickerAnchor` from the committed cutoff and loads the picker month (ViewModel:152-162); day taps update only the draft (:165-167); shortcuts move the draft and the picker month while the anchor stays on the committed cutoff (:170-181, asserted); month navigation re-anchors on the month's first day (:187-197, asserted); confirm commits the draft, leaves Today-following, clears all transient picker fields and the previous result, and shows loading while the new date resolves (:203-227, asserted); cancel clears every transient field and preserves the result (:230-242, asserted); stale picker-month responses are discarded by `pickerMonthRequestId` plus the `pickerMonth` key (:387-455). The Jan 13/16/22/24/25, Feb 7 and Jan 4 2027 reference values match the acceptance table exactly.
- **State-dependent BalanceScreen layout.** The four shapes match the brief's rules and the recorded mockup verdicts: Today shows the top **Choose date** primary action, the count heading and **Back to Calendar**; dated/projected shows only the secondary **Choose date** at the bottom; a period end shows the period card plus the primary **Choose date** at the bottom with no count heading and no projected note; unavailable shows the warning card with **Retry** + **Choose date** and the "Requested date · …" subtitle. Loading is keyed (`balanceLoading && (balanceFor != balanceDate \|\| balance == null)`), so a confirmed date never shows the previous date's figures.
- **BalancePresentation rules.** Ordering annual → sick → comp → credit → travel comp → time-off award → restored annual → religious comp → PPL → FMLA remaining matches chapter 01-02; annual/sick are kept even at zero, other categories only when nonzero (negatives and fractional values preserved); PPL/FMLA come only from `ParentalSummary`, an exhausted grant keeps its zero, a missing field renders **Unavailable**, and the rows are omitted when no parental summary exists ("leave omitted categories out"). Remaining unknown categories follow in sorted (stable-key) order, the spec's fallback when the source supplies no order. Hint math (`preview = columns × 2`, `hidden = size − visible`, clamped at zero) yields exactly "View all 10 balances · 2 more" for the ten-value double.
- **Balance card.** One 48 dp+ clickable surface opens BALANCES; the merged contentDescription announces the header, each previewed short label + value, the hidden-count hint and the **Open balances and date** action, per callout 1; keyed loading (spinner when the key mismatches or no result exists) continues the I05-02 F2 fix.
