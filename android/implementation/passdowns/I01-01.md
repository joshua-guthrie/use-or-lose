## Environment notes

- SDK lives in `~/Android/Sdk`; `android/local.properties` points to it and stays untracked.
- The host has 5.6 GB RAM. Stop the Gradle daemon before booting the emulator; under memory pressure the emulator shows "System UI isn't responding" — tap **Wait** and continue. A physical phone would be more comfortable for repeated UI work.
- The app was already configured with `android:allowBackup="false"` and `android:fullBackupContent="false"`.
